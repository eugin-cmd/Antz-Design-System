# Proposal for a change to the Antz Design System

Use this when a need cannot be met with existing tokens, components or patterns. Fill it in, save it in the project (for example `proposals/RFC-short-name.md`) and send it to the System Owner. Until it is approved, use the closest approved option and mark it with a comment naming the proposal.

Classes: **patch** (fix), **minor** (new token, variant or component, nothing removed), **major** (something removed or renamed), **foundational** (brand colour, typeface, principles, governance).

---
id: RFC-NNN
title: <short name>
author: <name>
date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
class: patch | minor | major | foundational
status: draft | in review | accepted | rejected | withdrawn
---

## The need
What problem does this solve, for whom, in which modules and screens? Include screenshots or links.

## What exists today
Which tokens, components or patterns did you check first? Why don't they meet the need?

## Proposal
What changes: tokens (names and values), components (variants, states), docs, platform outputs.

## Alternatives considered
At least one alternative and why it was not chosen.

## Accessibility
Contrast in light, dark and high contrast; keyboard; screen reader; touch targets; motion.

## Impact
Affected components, modules and apps. Breaking? Migration steps and codemod if so.

## Decision
Filled in by the approvers: decision, date, approvers, resulting decision record (if any) and target version.
