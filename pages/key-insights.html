Key insights

A white card with a period switch over three boxed figures (Figma "Key Insights": Today, Yesterday, This month). Switching the tab swaps the figures in place. The figures sit on the Primary wash, the one for general summaries (DD-001).

Key insights
24Births
6Transfers
1Deaths

Key insights

Do
  • Keep three figures per period.
  • Keep the figures in the same order across tabs.
Don't
  • Offer more than three periods.
  • Let the card change height when the tab changes.
