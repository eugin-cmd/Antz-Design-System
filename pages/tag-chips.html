Tag chips

Labels on animals, species and places. The shape says who can see the tag: a pill is public, a tag shape is private. The colour and its marks say the priority, on the same scale as the priority picker.

Public and private, by priority

A pill is a public tag; the tag shape, with its point, is a private one. Priority uses the priority picker's scale: Normal ! on info, High !! on caution (amber) and Critical !!! on danger. A tag with no priority yet is neutral grey. The marks are always shown, so priority never depends on colour alone.

Parts and states

A private tag someone else added starts with their initials (their name is in the tooltip and is read out). A removable chip ends with a remove button; a tag being created gets the small New tag beside it. Clickable chips are buttons that open the tag; in history and previews they are static.

Long names

A chip never grows wider than its container: a long name ends with an ellipsis, and the full name is still read out.

Tag chips

Do
  • Use the pill for public tags and the tag shape for private ones.
  • Use the priority scale: no priority, Normal, High, Critical.
  • Keep the mark on every chip that has a priority.
  • Show who added a private tag that is not yours.
Don't
  • Pick a colour per category or per tag.
  • Use teal, or any hue, to mean "no priority".
  • Show priority by colour alone.
  • Put a filled main button inside a chip list.
