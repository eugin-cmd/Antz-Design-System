Responsive

Do
  • Design the phone layout first, then add columns and the rail as space grows.
  • Put app layouts in .shell-host so they follow their container.
  • Check 390, 820 and 1280 in both themes.
Don't
  • Stretch the phone layout across a desktop.
  • Let anything scroll sideways; tables and code scroll inside their wrapper.
  • Hide content people need on phones; stack it instead.
