Dashboard widgets

The pieces of the Antz home dashboard and module home pages: photo heroes, widgets with charts and period pills, status tiles, quick add, photo tiles, the week agenda, the module grid and more.

Photo hero

A real photograph with a side scrim (--gradient-scrim-side) so white text reads, a glass tag, the title, and up to three figures in glass icon squares. The compact version leads a module home, with figures in glass chips.

Widgets

A head (icon, title, subtitle, and an arrow or period pills), one big figure, and its detail: a bar chart, inset stat tiles or a split. Five fills: --sky and --teal (vivid), --ink (dark, warm figure), --mint and --glow (light), plus plain. Turn on edit mode to show the remove badges.

Dashboard widgets

Do
  • Use one photo hero per screen, with a real photograph.
  • Give each widget one figure people act on, and a way into the module.
  • Mix fills for rhythm, but keep each module on the same fill everywhere.
  • Use status tiles only for counts that need action.
Don't
  • Put status on a vivid fill or a gradient: use a status tile.
  • Make the ticker move by itself: moving text must be pausable, so it scrolls only when touched.
  • Put more than six bars in a widget chart; link to the report instead.
  • Use photo tiles without alt text naming the animal.
