Role homes

The home adapts to what each person can use: a panel hero for one module, a board for several, the Hospital split card, and the quick dock.

Panel hero, split card and quick dock

The panel hero heads a one-module home: a title, a period switch and figures with icon tiles (a danger tile for what needs action). The split card puts a photo widget, a short list and status tiles side by side. The quick dock floats Quick actions and chat at the foot of the board.

Role homes

Do
  • Give a one-module person a home for that module, not an empty board.
  • Use the danger icon tile only for figures that need action.
Don't
  • Show the quick dock and a FAB on the same screen.
  • Put more than three figures in a panel hero.
