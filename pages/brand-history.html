Brand history

Do
  • Change the brand only through a foundational decision (FD) approved by the System Owner; it ships as a new release.
  • Compare two versions by opening both releases from the table.
Don't
  • Edit a published release to change its brand: releases never change.
  • Use a retired value, even to match an old screen.
