Pattern components

Navigation drawer

The main menu, opened from the hamburger: a rail of modules beside that module's menu. Each module has its own soft tone, so people always know where they are.

Navigation drawer

Opens from the hamburger at the left of the top bar. The rail lists the modules; the panel shows the chosen module's menu, with a search, optional group headings, and the zoo switcher and user at the foot. The white rail parts around the active module, so its tab opens into the panel, and the panel takes on that module's tone. Switching modules fades from one tone to the next.

Anatomy

  • Rail (96px): one tab per module, icon over label. Up and down arrows move between tabs.
  • Panel: search, the menu (items with icons, optional overline group headings) and the foot (zoo switcher, user, notifications). The current page has aria-current="page".
  • Tone: data-tone on each tab; the drawer takes the selected tab's tone.

Module tones

Seven soft tones, each a tint pair and an ink. They are for identity only: never status, never data. The suggested modules are a default: give each product module one tone and keep it everywhere (drawer, module band, launcher tile).

Every tone's ink passes 4.5:1 on its tints, and body and secondary text pass on every tint, in light, dark and both high-contrast modes. The build checks this.

Module band

A soft strip behind a module page's header, in the module's tone, fading into the page. It echoes the drawer, so the page and the menu agree. Put data-tone on the band or on the page.

Diet items

Navigation drawer

Do
  • Use one drawer for the whole product, opened from the hamburger.
  • Give each module one tone and use it in the drawer, the module band and its launcher tile.
  • Group long menus with overline headings (Operations, Admin).
  • Keep rail labels short: about 11 characters fit.
Don't
  • Use a tone to mean status.
  • Put add or edit forms in this drawer: they belong in the right work drawer.
  • Nest menus more than one level.
  • Add a new tone locally: propose it.
