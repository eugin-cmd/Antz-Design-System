Components

Side sheets

Panels that slide in from the right for adding, editing and looking closely at a record, beside the list they belong to. Attached to the edge, or floating with rounded corners.

Floating side sheet

The work drawer, set in from the top, right and bottom edges with large rounded corners (.drawer--floating). It reads like a dialog that slides in, but keeps the list visible beside it. Use it for a record's details and for add and edit forms. A header with a meta line and a large title, a scrolling body, and a footer with the actions, split evenly.

Parts for detail sheets

  • Record hero (.record-hero): the lead card on the green gradient: where it came from, two to three key figures, and the person responsible with quick actions (.icon-btn--on-strong).
  • Party card (.party): a person or animal on the record, with a label, name and quick actions. .party--tint for people, the default sunken card for animals, .party--plain inside the hero.
  • Outlined row (.row--outlined with .row__qty): an item on the record with its quantity.
  • Section heading (.drawer__section) and split footer (.drawer__foot--split).

Work drawer (attached)

The original form: full height, flush to the right edge (.drawer, 562px). Use it on dense desktop screens where the sheet stays open while people work through a list. Both forms share every part and behaviour; pick one per product.

Side sheets

Do
  • Use a side sheet to add, edit or inspect one record beside its list.
  • Keep one main action in the footer; put the secondary action beside it.
  • Lead a detail sheet with one record hero, then the people and items.
  • Close on the close button, the backdrop and Escape, and return focus to what opened it.
Don't
  • Stack a sheet on a sheet, or open a dialog for something a sheet can hold.
  • Use the floating and attached forms in the same product.
  • Put more than one record hero in a sheet.
  • Use a sheet for a quick confirmation: that is a dialog.
