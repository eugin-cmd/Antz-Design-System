Antz Design Systemv1.2.0-beta.5 · Versions
Foundations

Motion gallery

Every motion in the system in one place: play it, slow it down, or preview it with motion turned off.

Play with every motion

Hover, click or replay each item. Slow motion stretches every duration four times, so overshoots and settles are easy to see. Reduced motion shows what people who turn motion off get: every change is instant. The gallery uses the real components, so what you see here is what ships.

Easing curves

The motion tokens as curves; the dot travels with each one. Anything that rises above the top line overshoots, then settles.

Micro-interactions

Small responses to hover, press and focus. Everything that moves uses --ease-move: a small overshoot, then settle.

Controls

Switches, tabs and pickers confirm a choice with a short, springy move.

Overlays

Things that open: they arrive with the elastic settle and leave quickly on --ease-exit. Panels attached to the screen edge don't overshoot.

Content and data

Content rises in, accordions open, and figures change. Data never overshoots, so a bar never shows a wrong value, even briefly.

Ambient and loading

Loops that show something is live or loading. They run at constant speed and stop under reduced motion.

Bigger motions on their own pages

The animal card's expand and collapse is on Animal card, and the navigation drawer with its module tone fade is on Navigation drawer. Durations, curves and the rules for choosing them are on Motion.
