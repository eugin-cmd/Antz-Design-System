Priority picker

Three radios drawn as marks: one, two or three exclamation marks, so the level never depends on colour alone.

Priority picker

Three radios drawn as marks: one, two or three exclamation marks, so the level never depends on colour alone. Normal is sky, high is amber and emergency is the danger red. Tag chips use the same scale and marks.

Priority

Priority picker

Do
  • Default to Normal.
  • Keep the exclamation marks so the level reads without colour.
Don't
  • Add a fourth level.
  • Use the emergency red outside the priority picker.
