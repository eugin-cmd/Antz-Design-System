Widget board

The home board people arrange themselves: the board bar, every widget fill with pills and remove badges, the hero widget, the Add widgets gallery, All modules, Today, task rows and notes.

Board bar

Menu (large module tiles with a pin on hover), the site picker, + to add widgets and the pencil to edit. Editing shows a remove badge on each widget, with Undo changes and Done.

Menu filter and account menu

Open both: the filter sits in the board menu, the account menu at the right end of the header. The board menu's filter panel (popover__panel) has single-choice chips for Show, Category and Sort, and a foot with Clear all and a counted Show button. The account menu starts with a flat summary of the person's access (gallery__default--flat).
