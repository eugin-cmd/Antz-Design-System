Quick add

One dialog for a note, a request or an announcement, opened from the quick add tiles. Tabs switch the form; the footer counts the required fields left.

Quick add dialog

Select Note, Request or Announcement to open the dialog. It uses the system's fields: choice chips, a text area, a date and selects in a row, the priority picker, a toggle row, a person field and the drop zone. Add stays disabled until the required fields are done, and the footer says how many are left.
