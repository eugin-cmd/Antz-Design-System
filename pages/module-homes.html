Pattern components

Module homes

The pieces every module home is built from: the band hero, the banner hero, class cards, the date scroller, the range bar, review rows with a route, deltas and the butter and maroon fills.

Heroes and pieces

A module home starts with one hero. The band hero is a dark band with rings, a live overline and one big figure, in the module's tone: lagoon (the default), navy, ink, wine or bronze. Its right side takes a glass button (or the bright one, for the main action), a came-in and went-out split or an avatar stack. Deltas point up or down. The banner hero is a photo with a big title and glass figures. Then come the widgets, lists and the module's own pieces.

  • Use a band tone to signal status.
