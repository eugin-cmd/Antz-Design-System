Pattern components

Record detail

What opens from a record list: the detail page (head, facts, tabbed tables) and the detail side sheet (hero card, contacts with call and chat, the items).

Detail head and facts

The detail head has a back link to the list, the record ID with its priority and status, and at most two actions. The facts card puts what people check first in one place: the lead fact large across two columns, then people, figures and dates.
