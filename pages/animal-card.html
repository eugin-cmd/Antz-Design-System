Animal card (compact and expanded)

One card, two states. Compact puts the photo beside the AAID, three facts and the age and weight pills, for lists and grids. Expanded puts the photo on top with the AAID and pills over a soft scrim, adds the latest alert, five facts and a footer of record counts with the way into the full record. The button on the photo switches between them, and the card morphs where the browser supports it.

What you can leave out

The sex tag, AAID and name are required. Everything else is optional: delete it from the markup and the card closes up. No photo shows the paw mark; no media block makes a text-only card; no button keeps the card in one state; no footer leaves no gap. Adding a part (a fifth count, a second alert, a new colour) needs a proposal, so every team keeps the same card. Full list: specs/components/animal-card.md.

Anatomy

  1. Photo, or the paw mark on a tint
  2. Sex tag and CITES appendix (short form when compact)
  3. Show more / show less button
  4. AAID: in the body when compact, over the photo when expanded
  5. Latest alert (expanded): the one fact that needs attention
  6. Facts: name, species, enclosure; expanded adds section and site
  7. Age and weight pills
  8. Footer (expanded): treatments, lab tests, vaccinations, medicines, and View details

Animal card (compact and expanded)

Do
  • Use compact in lists and grids; expand one card at a time, or open it expanded on a record page.
  • Show one alert line, the most important one, in the warning colour; use danger only for mortality.
  • Give every photo alt text naming the animal; keep the AAID on the scrim, never straight on the photo.
  • Label the counts for screen readers (they are icons with numbers).
Don't
  • Add more than five facts or four counts.
  • Put coloured text straight on a photo.
  • Show both the compact pills and the photo pills at once.
  • Hide the AAID: it is the animal's identity.

Animal card and sex tags

Do
  • Show the AAID and the sex tag on every animal card.
  • Mark a death with the mortality tag and the red date line; keep the line visible in both states.
Don't
  • Run a stripe down the card's outer edge.
  • Show more than four facts on one card.

Animal card (compact and expanded)

Set data-state to compact or expanded. Elements with .ac-c show only when compact, .ac-x only when expanded. A button with data-animal-toggle switches state and keeps aria-expanded in step.

      <span class="animal-card__badge"><span class="ac-x">CITES I</span><span class="ac-c">C I</span></span>
    </div>
    <div class="animal-card__overlay ac-x"><b class="animal-card__id">AAID: 87546/24</b>
