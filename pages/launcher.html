Pattern components

Launcher and headers

The Home launcher of round module tiles under a gradient headline, and the dark entity header for a species, animal or enclosure.

Launcher

Shortcuts to the pages people use most, as round module tiles with a label under each. Tiles take their module's launch gradient, so a page has the same colour here as its module's tone. Put "All menus" first, then a divider. Shown here on a module band with the display headline.

What will you work on today?

Launch gradients

Five gradients, one per module family: --gradient-launch-green, -amber, -sky, -slate and -coral. Every stop keeps the white icon at 3:1 or more. The plain tile (.tile-module--plain) is for "All menus".

Display headline gradient

One headline per screen can run from deep teal through teal to brand green (.t-gradient, --gradient-text-brand). Large text only: every stop passes 3:1. In high contrast and forced colours it falls back to the normal text colour.

Entity header

Heads the detail view of a species, animal or enclosure: picture, name, scientific name and ID, a cluster of key figures and a few quiet actions, on the petrol gradient (--gradient-petrol). Tabs for the record's sections sit directly under it.

Launcher and headers

Do
  • Show up to about ten shortcuts; "All menus" opens the navigation drawer.
  • Match each tile's gradient to its module's tone.
  • Use one gradient headline per screen, at display size.
  • Keep the entity header to three or four figures and two or three actions.
Don't
  • Use launch gradients as status or on buttons.
  • Put body text or small labels in the gradient.
  • Put a main (filled green) action in the entity header: it goes in the page's action bar.
  • Use the petrol gradient for anything but entity headers.
