# ANTZ Line animated (proposal)

An outline style for the Primary (module) icons, proposed on 24 November 2026 at the System Owner's request, after
the "lineal colour" animated icons on Flaticon (style reference only: every drawing here is original).

- 48px grid, live area 4 to 44, 1.5px round strokes, no fills: slim, even lines with fine secondary detail. Use at 48 to 72px.
- Ink: `--color-text`, so it follows light, dark and high contrast when the SVG is inline.
- One accent detail per icon, a gradient from `--scale-primary-600` (#21924E) to `--scale-secondary-600` (#00A698):
  each end passes 3:1 as a graphic on light and dark surfaces.
- Overlaps: an element in front (a magnifier, a pencil, a stamp) knocks out the lines behind it with a 1.5px gap,
  through a mask rather than a white fill, so it reads as solid on any background and the cut-out moves with it.
- Motion: the outline draws on (`--ease-out`), the accent pops or moves with the elastic settle (`--ease-elastic`),
  once (2.8s), then the icon rests on its final frame. No loops (WCAG 2.2.2). Replay on hover, focus or tap with
  `data-icon-replay` (components.js). `static/` has every icon with no motion. Reduced motion shows the final frame.
- Where to use them: DD-004 (proposed): module navigation, empty states, onboarding, confirmations, at 48 to 72px.

Generated by `node tools/primary-line.mjs` (drawings live in that file): all 30 Primary (module) icons, matching the
Tile set, plus 28 interface icons with micro-animations (`set: "ui"` in index.json): search, refresh, download,
upload, success, delete, favourite, calendar, send, lock, visibility, location, alert, star, camera, add, filter,
sort, edit, share, copy, print, log out, link, attach, microphone, folder, bookmark; and 12 animal care icons
(`set: "care"`): vaccine, pill, vital signs, temperature, weight, water, weather, birds, fish, transport, scan tag, map. Not yet reviewed by the ANTZ icon team; Roshni's tile icons in `../primary/` are unchanged.
