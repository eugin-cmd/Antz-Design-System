Pickers, switcher and split button

Select a trigger to open its panel. Pickers mark the chosen option and update the label. The module switcher shows every module in a compact grid. The split button pairs one action with a menu of related ones.

Sort menu

A picker on the sort button of a list toolbar: Newest first, Oldest first, Species A–Z and AAID. Each order has its icon from ANTZ Rounded (i-sort-newest, i-sort-oldest, i-sort-az); choosing one updates the trigger's label and icon (data-icon on the option, data-pick-icon on the trigger icon).

Popovers and pickers

Do
  • Use the site picker in the module bar and the board bar.
  • Keep the chosen option visible in the trigger.
Don't
  • Put forms in a popover: use the work drawer.
  • Open a popover on hover.
