Hero options

Two ways to head a module page with a photo. The foot bar (hero-photo--foot, hero-photo__foot) runs along the bottom with a meta item and a link, and the figures spread across the width (hero-photo__stats--spread). The site hero (hero-photo--site) adds a back chip, an overline, the person in charge and a tag, with the figures in a glass panel on the right.
