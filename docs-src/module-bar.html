Module bar and side panel

The module home (modhome) stacks the module bar over the body: the side panel and the main column. The module switcher opens a grid of every module. The breadcrumb shows on desktop. The side panel groups pages under overline headings, shows counts (danger counts in red), opens sub-pages and folds to icons. A section head puts pickers beside a title.
